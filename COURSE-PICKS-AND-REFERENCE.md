**What to look for in each (this becomes the skill's observation checklist):**
* **Hair / braid:** the 3-strand interlock direction • how the braid tightens at
  the scalp and loosens toward the tail • the **specular band** that runs *along*
  the braid axis • flyaway halo silhouette • where flyaways break the outline.
* **Fabric / athletic lycra:** **tension folds** (long, pulled, direction follows
  the stretch) vs **compression folds** (bunched at joints) vs **bunching** •
  lycra stretches *with* the body → few folds, broad soft sheen • fold count drops
  as tension rises.

**Better than stills:** slow-motion video of the real material. That's exactly the
method CG Cookie's fabric course uses — and it's what Demers/Gurney (already in
your skill) demand for observation.
